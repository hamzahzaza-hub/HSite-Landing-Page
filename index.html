Features

What Hsite will do

Everything you need to find classmates, bring in your academic record, stay on track for graduation, and build things outside of class, in one app.

Study partners and groups

Find students in your major and your classes, then create or join a study group for a specific course.

Class planner

Upload your degree audit or unofficial transcript, or start from your major's requirements, instead of typing in every class. See what's done, what's left, and plan the semesters ahead.

Transfer plans

Upload your transfer credit evaluation to see which courses came in as a direct match, which counted as electives, and which didn't transfer. Connect with students who transferred from the same schools.

Goals

Set your target graduation semester, plans to transfer, career interests, and limits like a work schedule. Hsite shapes your semester plan around them.

Side project groups

Pitch an idea or join someone else's, and find teammates with the skills your project needs.

Major chat

Talk with students in your major about classes, professors, and what to take next.

Potential

Where Hsite could take you

Beyond classes and planning, Hsite is a way to meet the students who care about the same things you do, and start something with them.

Potential

Work on projects that excite more than just you

Share an idea and see who else is interested. Build the projects that catch other students' attention too, not just your own.

Potential

Find peers with similar interests

Connect with students who share your interests, whether that's game design, robotics, health tech, or something no one else in your classes is into.

Potential

Research together

Team up with students curious about the same questions, share sources and findings, and explore a topic more deeply than you could alone.

Potential

Build something together

Turn a shared interest into something real, like an app, a club, a competition entry, or a portfolio project you made as a team.

How it works

From sign-up to study group

  1. Pick your school and major

    Join with your school and major so Hsite can connect you with the right students.

  2. Bring in your record

    Upload your degree audit, transcript, or transfer credit evaluation, or pick your major's requirements. Check the list, then set your graduation goal. Your grades stay private.

  3. Team up

    Join study groups, chat with your major, and start or join a side project.
